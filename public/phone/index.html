SENTRYSHIELD SENSOR

This phone becomes the acoustic sensor: the speaker emits 17.5–22 kHz chirps and the microphone listens for echoes. Everything is processed on the device.

Chirps are near-ultrasonic and quiet, but audible to some people and to pets. Research prototype — not a certified navigation or safety device.
